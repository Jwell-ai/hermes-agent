# Picture-Book Visual Styles

Choose one primary style and keep it throughout the book. Do not stack unrelated styles.

## Warm Hand-Drawn Picture Book

- Good for bedtime, growth, family, nature, gentle stories.
- Soft watercolor or colored-pencil texture, light paper grain, clear composition.
- Low-saturation warm palette: cream, pale blue, grass green, soft yellow, coral.
- Gentle irregular linework and clear facial expressions.
- Avoid excessive stickers, harsh lighting, poster composition, and overly complex backgrounds.

## Paper-Textured Playful Hand Drawing

Use the "paper-textured playful hand-drawn" idea, but reduce PPT/infographic traits:

- Keep: light gray-white paper texture, thin black outline, low-saturation pastel colors, relaxed whitespace.
- Reduce: contents pages, module cards, arrows, presentation title hierarchy.
- Strengthen: continuous scenes, character actions, emotional changes, story props.

## Clean Whiteboard / Simple Line Story

- Background: white or very light paper.
- Subject: black/gray linework with small blue/orange accents.
- Good for concept picture books, science explainers, and simple line stories.
- Do not turn it into a flowchart. Every page must be a story scene.

## Minimal Quirky Character Book

- A minimal character can be a protagonist or companion, but must drive the story.
- Keep white or pale backgrounds, clear action, and generous whitespace.
- Quirky is fine; scary, oppressive, or adult-coded is not.
- Do not copy existing cases, compositions, props, or IP.
- Do not make every page an article illustration. Keep continuous story rhythm.

## Folk / Eastern Hand-Drawn Style

- Good for traditional stories, seasons, family memory, mountains/water.
- Rice-paper texture, ink linework, soft color washes.
- Palette: ink, cinnabar, blue-green, cream.
- Keep children's books bright; avoid old, yellowed, or dark visual tone.

## Prohibited

- No copyrighted characters, film characters, brand IP, or obvious imitations.
- No realistic child photos or photoreal child portraits.
- No horror, gore, sexualized content, punishment violence, or adult imagery.
- Do not let text dominate the image.
- Do not make one book a random style collection.

## Style Prompt Format

```text
Style: warm children's picture-book illustration, soft watercolor and colored-pencil texture, subtle paper grain, low-saturation warm palette, delicate hand-drawn linework, clean whitespace, gentle readable expressions, suitable for ages 3-8.
```

## Page-Type Rotation

Do not make every page a full-bleed cinematic scene. Mix page types while preserving style:

- full scene: world, atmosphere, turning point
- whitespace action page: one action or expression
- small-panel sequence: repetition, practice, feedback
- object close-up: clue, cause/effect, discovery
- echo page: parallel composition between beginning and ending

The first goal of every page is readable action and emotion, not a packed background.
